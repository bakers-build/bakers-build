# Skills & Workflows

**Your project decides how agents work. bakers.build keeps every ticket on track.**

Each ticket follows a workflow: a fixed series of states such as `plan`, `working` and `review`. In each state, one skill tells the agent what to do. The `WORKFLOW.md` in your repository picks those skills, your engineering method, your checks and your standards.

- [How it works](/skills-and-workflows/how-it-works): from a ticket to a finished step.
- [Configure your workflow](/skills-and-workflows/write-workflow-md): shape `WORKFLOW.md` for your project.
- [Workflows and states](/skills-and-workflows/workflows-and-states): what happens in each state.
- [Labels and work types](/skills-and-workflows/labels-and-work-types): labels that choose skills, methods and agents.

::: tip Ask your agent
Explain which skill owns each state of this project's workflows, and check that its WORKFLOW.md is valid.
:::
