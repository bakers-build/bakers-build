# Work in the browser

**Create tickets in your chat, follow and accept them on the web. Nothing to install.**

**You need:** a bakers.build account. A workspace Admin invites you.

## Two windows

| Window | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Your chat:** claude.ai, Claude Desktop or Cowork with the bakers.build connector | Describe what you need. Claude writes the ticket, checks for duplicates, qualifies it and takes it to `spec`. |
| **bakers.build on the web:** your team's bakers.build address, for example `https://app.bakers.build` | See every ticket and its progress, read summaries, freeze or cancel, record your verdict, record time. |

The building happens elsewhere: your team's agents pick the ticket up and leave a `summary` after every step. You read, decide and continue.

## Your first ticket

1. [Connect your chat](/daily-work/claude-ai): five minutes, once.
2. In your Project, write: `Create a bakers.build ticket for <what you need and why>.` Confirm when Claude asks.
3. Open the ticket on the web. Watch its state and read each `summary`.
4. When the work is ready, [accept it](/daily-work/accept-results) with **Accept** or **Reject** on the ticket.

**Done when:** your ticket is in `spec` and you can open it on the web.

## Finding your way around

The left rail lists your workspace's projects, teams and time. The workspace switcher at the top changes the workspace; the account avatar opens your own pages: time, schedule, API keys, and **Back to workspace**. Workspaces you are not a member of do not appear anywhere.

## Good to know

When an agent needs a decision during a step, it asks on the machine that runs the ticket. Your teammate there answers or passes the question to you; a comment you write on the ticket reaches the next step. [Follow a ticket](/daily-work/follow-a-ticket#where-you-answer) explains it.
