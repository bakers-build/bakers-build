# Record time

**Your agent proposes your hours. You review them and save. Only saved time counts.**

**You need:** Contributor rank in the workspace.

1. **Ask your agent.**

   ```text
   Propose my attributable activities for this week with concrete durations, then give me the review link.
   ```

   Your agent considers holidays, illness and other exceptions. Its **proposal** books nothing: only you see it, marked **Proposed**.

2. **Review it.** The link opens a table with activities as rows and dates as columns. Adjust durations in the cells (`1:30` is ninety minutes) and expand a row to change description, project or ticket.

3. **Save.** **Save time** records the whole proposal at once; saving twice never books twice. **Discard proposal** removes it.

4. **Add time yourself.** **Record time** (the plus button) asks for project, optional ticket, date, duration and description. The billing account comes from the project or ticket; you can pick another active account of that project.

## Where you see your time

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| Account → **Time** | Your hours in all workspaces |
| **My time** | Your hours in this workspace, by day or week |
| Team → **Time** | The team's recorded hours; team leads only |
| Project → **Time** | Your hours on the project, plus your team's if you lead it |

## Who sees what

| Who | Sees your entries | Edits them |
|---|---|---|
| You | All, including proposals | Yes |
| A lead of your team | Recorded entries in that team's workspace | No |
| Anyone else | No | No |

Managing billing accounts gives no access to other people's hours. Your weekly schedule under Account → **Schedule** is private planning input for your agent.

**Done when:** your saved entries match your work.
