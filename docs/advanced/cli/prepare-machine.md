# Bring your tooling

**Your machine. Your tools. More workers when you need them.**

**New here? [Install the CLI first →](/advanced/cli/install)**, then pick your tooling here.

## Start local. Scale out.

- **Developer computer:** run bakers.build alongside your existing setup.
- **Remote workers:** add servers when you need more capacity.
- **Just follow tickets:** let your team's workers do the work.

## Choose your workspace manager

Keep terminals organized and agent sessions running.

- **[herdr — available now](/advanced/cli/herdr).** bakers.build's current workspace-manager integration.
- **tuios — coming soon.** Another workspace-manager integration is on the way.

[Workspace setup and checks →](/advanced/cli/check-machine-tools)

## Bring your development environment

**[DDEV](/advanced/cli/ddev), [Node.js](/advanced/cli/node#node-js-projects), Docker or your own tooling.** Your project decides.

- **Use what works:** keep your existing setup and scripts.
- **Connect through hooks:** prepare each ticket's environment with project commands.
- **Local or remote:** those commands can also reach a remote environment.

[Connect your tooling with hooks →](/advanced/configuration/hooks)

## Pick your agent

**Claude Code, Codex, Grok, Kimi, OpenCode or Pi.** Choose the agents your workers use.

[Agent setup →](/advanced/configuration/agents-and-models)
