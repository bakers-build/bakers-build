# Outtake at review

Read by [the outtake](../outtake.md) for a review verdict.

- **Outcome** names the verdict, `OK` or `Not OK`.
- **Problems**: open finding IDs and required repair; cite the verdict under
  [ticket writes](../ticket-writes.md).
- **Success**: reused evidence by check ID, each with its original revision, separate from fresh
  results; AC IDs and passing criteria stay in the typed verdict, not in a visible list.
- **Manual review**: what remains open for the human, with persona, environment and the MR link.
- **Next action**: on `OK` the merge decision; on `Not OK` the route the verdict's causes give.
